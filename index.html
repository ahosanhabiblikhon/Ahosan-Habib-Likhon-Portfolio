About Me

Who I Am

Passionate about biology, technology, and the intersection of both

I'm Ahosan Habib Likhon, a dedicated Biotechnology & Genetic Engineering student at Islamic University, Bangladesh. My journey combines the fascinating world of biological sciences with the innovative realm of technology.

My academic background in biotechnology has given me a deep understanding of genetic engineering, molecular biology, and bioinformatics. I'm particularly interested in how computational tools and artificial intelligence can revolutionize biological research and medical discoveries.

Beyond the laboratory and coding, I express my creativity through photography, capturing moments that tell stories. This artistic pursuit complements my scientific mindset, teaching me to observe details and patterns in both nature and technology.
